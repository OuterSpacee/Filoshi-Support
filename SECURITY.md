# Security policy

## Reporting a security problem

**Please do not report security problems in a public issue or discussion.** Email [support@filoshi.com](mailto:support@filoshi.com) with the subject line starting with `SECURITY:`.

Include:

- what is affected (the Filoshi desktop app with its version from Settings → About, the installer, the auto-updater, or filoshi.com and the page address),
- what an attacker could do,
- the steps to reproduce it, and any proof-of-concept,
- whether you want to be credited, and under which name.

We will confirm we received your report within 3 business days, keep you updated while we work on it, and tell you when a fix is released. Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it publicly.

## What is in scope

- The Filoshi desktop app (Windows 2.0.x, macOS and Linux 1.0.36) and its installer.
- The auto-update feed and downloads served from filoshi.com.
- The filoshi.com website.

Problems in the bundled open-source engines (FFmpeg, ImageMagick, Pandoc, PDFium, pdfcpu, 7-Zip) or the LibreOffice and FreeCAD add-ons are welcome too; we may also point you to the upstream project.

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| 2.0.x (Windows) | Yes |
| 1.0.36 (macOS, Linux) | Yes, until 2.x is released for these platforms |
| Older 1.0.x | No, please update |
